Geo3d Search Sample (Aircraft)

Live aircraft positions plotted on a 3D globe (CesiumJS). Demonstrates laurus' geo3d field type with ECEF bounding-box and nearest-neighbour queries — true 3D, including altitude.

First-visit downloads: UniDic dictionary (~52 MB, shared with the other samples) and the CesiumJS bundle (~5 MB) from unpkg with SRI hashes. Live aircraft data comes from the community ADS-B feed at airplanes.live.

Search

Text input is wrapped in the unified DSL. Examples: callsign:JAL*, aircraft_type:B38M, description:Boeing, category:heavy. Toggle a 3D constraint below to combine the text with a spatial query. The two constraints are mutually exclusive.

With "Filter by 3D bbox" on, the demo emits +(text) +position:geo3d_bbox(minX, minY, minZ, maxX, maxY, maxZ) derived from a 200 km AABB centred on the camera ECEF position. With "Nearest" on, it emits position:geo3d_nearest(targetX, targetY, targetZ, 20) where the target is the screen-centre intersection with the WGS84 ellipsoid.
